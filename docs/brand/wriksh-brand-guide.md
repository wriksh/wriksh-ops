# Wriksh brand guide

A short reference for anyone writing copy, designing slides, or styling web pages for WRIKSH — the portrayal of the Suvarna Yuga of Bharath.

## Voice

- Warm, grounded, lightly poetic.
- Avoid marketing fluff ("exciting", "revolutionary", "transformative").
- Reference the philosophy of the Suvarna Yuga of Bharath sparingly.
- Always centre the practitioners — they are the protagonists, not us.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| linen | `#F4ECDD` | page background |
| parchment | `#EFE6D2` | cards |
| stone | `#C9B98C` | borders, muted ink |
| ink | `#1B1812` | primary text |
| gold | `#C8932F` | accent — buttons, eyebrows, gold rules |
| clay | `#B8512C` | warnings, secondary action |
| forest | `#2D4521` | primary actions, dark surfaces |
| moss | `#5C7B3F` | success, finance income |
| rust | `#C7603A` | finance expense, urgency |
| umber | `#6E4B25` | secondary text, footnotes |

## Typography

- **Spectral** (serif) — primary. Headings, body, all eyebrows.
- **Inter** (sans) — fallback for micro-UI only.

Avoid mixing. If a page feels busy, drop the sans.

## Logo

- Wordmark: `WRIKSH · DISCOVER · BOOK`
- Always in gold (`#C8932F`) over linen, or linen over forest.
- Never distort, recolour, or place over a busy image.

## The pillar words

- **Discover** — booking platform for traditional performances.
- **Experience** — curated regional trips.
- **Learn** — long-form apprenticeship programs.

These three are the spine of every page. Keep them visible in that order.
